About me

Designing with empathy,
building for everyone.

I'm a UX Designer and Researcher whose approach to design is human-centered, sustainable, functional, and beautiful. People and their pain points are at the heart of my design process as active participants, and not only as end users.

As the old saying goes,
If a tree falls in a forest and no one is around to hear it, does it make a sound?
I raise you,
If a solution is designed and cannot be accessed, does it actually exist?
