Case Study 01

Saffron & More. Brand Identity Guidelines & Hi-Fi Prototype

A bilingual brand identity system and mobile shopping prototype for Saffron & More, a bulk dry-goods store in Ann Arbor, Michigan.

Adobe Illustrator Figma Brand Identity UI Design Hi-Fi Prototype

01 · Client Brief

The Client Story

Saffron & More is a small bulk dry-goods store in Ann Arbor, Michigan, run by clients Fan and Liam. The store sells a range of goods including spices, nuts, seeds, grains, legumes, healing herbs, oils, coffee, tea, and candy, all sourced with quality and sustainability in mind. Fan and Liam came up with the tagline, "Dry Goods for All," to welcome Ann Arbor residents and University of Michigan students to try bulk shopping.

A distinguishing feature of the brand is its bilingual identity. Fan wanted to celebrate the Chinese name for saffron — 藏红花 (Zàng Hóng Huā) — by running the English and Chinese names together as a visual and cultural statement, highlighting the store's heritage and community.

"The brand should feel approachable and welcoming to all customers, regardless of their knowledge of bulk goods." — Client Brief

Target Audience

Ann Arbor residents and University of Michigan students who are environmentally conscious, prefer to shop at stores that promote sustainable practices, and seek a convenient local source for high-quality, specialty ingredients, while actively reducing their packaging waste.

Brand Personality

The brand should feel organic, natural, simple yet elegant, modern, and minimalist; conveying a sense of high-quality, sustainably sourced products.

Organic Natural Minimalist Modern Elegant Sustainable Approachable

Logo Brief

Simple yet memorable. Modern, minimalist, clean lines. Versatile across print, web, and packaging. Must include a bilingual variant pairing the English name with the Chinese 藏红花.

05 · Scalability & Free Space

Logo Usage Rules

Precise guidelines governing minimum sizes, variant selection by width, and the mandatory clear space that protects the logo's integrity across every application.

Grids and hierarchy were applied throughout the identity system. Each logotype variant is governed by width thresholds. The stacked logo is used at 1″ or wider, while the extended at 4″ or wider, ensuring consistent brand presence from a business card to a storefront banner.

07 · Hi-Fi Prototype

Figma Mobile Prototype

I designed a high-fidelity iOS prototype from the brand's UI identity, with a full style guide spanning atoms, molecules, and organisms.

Design Philosophy: The app was designed to meet Saffron & More's expectations of being "organic, natural, simple yet elegant, modern, and minimalist." The design leverages readability, efficiency, and minimalism throughout.

Typography: The Inter typeface was selected for its sleek legibility and suitability for user interfaces. All headings use 8-point incremental font sizes, consistent with design system conventions.

Navigation: A bottom tab bar with Home, Scan, Shop, and Account provides clear wayfinding. Hierarchy is maintained through size, weight, and Persian red active-state indicators.

Color: Vibrant Violet dominates interactive elements (buttons, CTAs). Persian Red signals active screen state in the tab bar. Barley provides a warm coupon background. Lilac is used for arrows, dividers, and the QR code background.

Grid & Hierarchy: The layout follows a consistent 8-point grid. Product cards, section headers, and list items are structured to create a clear visual hierarchy, guiding users from browsing to purchase with minimal friction.

Final Screens

The complete iOS prototype spans seven distinct screens representing the full user journey from login to checkout.
